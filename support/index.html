Getting started

What does Dupe do?

You make a character, then create realistic photos, carousels and short videos of it. Pick a preset (a ready-made look) or type your own idea. You can also Recreate a photo you upload with your character in it, Edit a result by describing a change, add an outfit or product photo, and add captions.

How do I make a character?

Two ways:

  • From your photos (free): add 3 clear photos of yourself, name the character, and tick the two boxes to confirm the photos are of you (or you have permission) and that you are 18 or over.
  • Character Studio (costs credits): invent a character by choosing their look. No photo of you is needed.

You can have up to 10 characters at a time.

Who can use Dupe?

Adults only. You must be 18 or over.

Credits

How do credits work?

Everything you create costs credits: photos, carousels, videos, edits and Character Studio characters. A single photo costs the least. Carousels and videos cost more, and longer videos cost more than short ones. The app always shows the cost before you confirm.

You get credits in two ways:

  1. Plan credits. Your plan gives you a fresh set of credits every period.
  2. Credit packs. One-off top-ups, available while you have a plan.

Do unused credits roll over?

Plan credits: no. At the start of each new period your plan credits reset to your plan's amount. Anything unused from the last period is gone.

Credit packs: they never expire. Unused pack credits stay on your account until you use them.

Which credits get used first?

Pack credits are used first, starting with the oldest pack. Your plan credits are used after that.

Can I use pack credits without a plan?

Yes. If your plan ends, your pack credits stay on your account and you can keep creating with them. They never expire. Only plan credits need an active plan.

I was charged credits but got nothing

Credits are taken when a generation starts. If it fails, times out or gets blocked by the AI's safety filter, the credits are returned automatically. You do not need to contact us. A stuck generation can take up to about 45 minutes to be marked as failed and refunded.

If a carousel only partly finishes, you keep the slides that were made, pay a reduced amount, and the rest of the credits come back.

If your credits have not come back after an hour, email us with the rough time of the generation and we will sort it out.

My credits came back as a "pack". Why?

Returned credits go back where they came from. Credits taken from a pack go back into that pack. If they came from a plan period that has since ended, they come back as a new pack, which never expires. You never lose returned credits.

Do I get credits back if I don't like a result?

If a generation fails, your credits come back automatically. AI results can vary, and some may look a little off. A result you don't love isn't refunded, but you can always try another take. Tip: clear, well-lit selfies give the best likeness. See "Getting better results" below.

When something goes wrong

Why was my prompt blocked?

Every idea you type, and every edit instruction, is checked before any credits are taken. The app tells you the reason. Prompts are blocked for sexual content, anything sexual involving a minor, intimate images of a real person, hate or harassment, graphic violence, illegal activity, or pretending to be a named real person or brand. Nights out, beaches, gyms and parties are fine. Try rewording your idea.

Why can I not type prompts for a day?

If many of your prompts are blocked (10 in 7 days), typed prompts are paused for 24 hours. Presets still work. The app shows when you can type prompts again. You get a warning first, after 5 blocked prompts in a day.

My account says it is suspended

Accounts are suspended straight away if a prompt is blocked for sexual content involving a minor. If you think this was a mistake, email support@joindupe.com from the address on your account and a person will review it.

Why did my generation fail?

The usual reasons:

  • The AI's safety filter blocked it. Sometimes it blocks things that seem harmless, such as certain outfits, poses or words. Try a different idea or preset.
  • The AI service was busy. We retry quietly a few times. If it still fails, try again in a few minutes.
  • It timed out. Videos and carousels take longer and occasionally do not finish.
  • Your character needs new photos. If you see a message about your character's photos, open the character and redo them.
  • Your connection dropped. A generation that already started carries on running on our servers, so check your gallery before trying again.

In every one of these cases your credits are returned automatically.

How long should it take?

About a minute for a photo or an edit, about 2 minutes for a carousel, and roughly 4, 6 or 8 minutes for a 5, 10 or 15-second video. You can leave the app. If notifications are on, we will tell you when it is ready. Results always appear in your Gallery either way.

I am not getting notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for Dupe in your iPhone's Settings (or from the app menu: Notifications). Your results always appear in your Gallery, whether or not the notification arrives.

Why can I only make one thing at a time?

To keep things fast and fair, each account runs one generation at a time. Start the next one when the current one is ready.

Getting better results

How do I make a good character from my photos?

Your 3 photos decide how much the results look like you. For the best likeness:

  • Good light. Face a window or step outside. Avoid harsh shadows across your face.
  • Just you. One person per photo, with your whole face clearly visible.
  • Nothing covering your face. No sunglasses, masks, or hats pulled low.
  • Mix it up. One straight on, one slightly turned, different expressions.
  • Sharp and recent. Not blurry, not heavily filtered, and how you look now.

The results do not look like me

Redo your character's photos using the tips above. The most common causes are filters, sunglasses, poor light and group photos. Redoing an existing character's photos costs 1 credit. The app tells you before you confirm.

Why was one of my photos rejected?

Every photo is checked automatically before it is used. Character photos must show one clear, real face (not a drawing or a screenshot of one). Any photo is rejected if the check finds sexual content, violence, hateful content, or anything sexual involving a minor. A rejected photo is deleted from our servers. Pick a different photo and try again. If you think the check got it wrong, email us.

Can I make a character of someone else?

Only if they are an adult and have clearly given you permission. Never upload photos of anyone under 18, and never upload photos of celebrities, public figures, or anyone who has not agreed. Accounts that break this rule are closed. See our Terms of Use.

Can I add my own outfit or product?

Yes. When you create, you can attach an outfit photo, a product photo, or both. They are saved to a library in your account so you can reuse them. Only upload photos you have the right to use.

What is Recreate?

Upload a photo of a scene you like, and Dupe makes a new version of it with your character in it. Only use photos you have the right to use, and never use Recreate to make it look as if a real person did something they did not.

Is there a limit?

Every account, with or without a plan, can have up to 10 characters, make up to 10 new characters from photos in any 24 hours, and check up to 20 outfit or product photos a day. Uploads must be JPEG or PNG and under 20 MB. The app tells you when you reach a limit and when you can try again.

Can I use my photos and videos for my business or social media?

Yes. Your results are yours to use, including commercially, as long as you follow our Terms of Use. Results are AI-made and carry no watermark, so please label them where a platform or the law asks you to. Many platforms, including Instagram and TikTok, ask you to label realistic AI-made content.

Your privacy

Is my face used to train AI?

No. We do not train AI models on your photos, your prompts or your results. The app does not build a custom AI model of you. Your photos are used as reference pictures at the moment each result is made.

To make your results, your photos and prompts are sent to the AI companies that run the models for us: WaveSpeed AI (and the model makers it uses) and Google (Gemini). You are asked for permission before this happens for the first time. The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Who can see my photos and results?

Only you. There is no public feed and other users cannot see your content. Your files are kept in private storage. We only look at content when you report it, when we investigate abuse, when you ask us to help with a problem, or where the law requires it.

How do I stop my photos being sent to AI?

Open the app menu and tap AI Photo Sharing, then withdraw your permission. New uploads and generations stop on all your devices. You can still open the app and view your Gallery. Your existing photos are not deleted by this. Use the delete options for that.

How do I delete a result, a character or a photo?

  • A result: open it in your Gallery and tap the delete (bin) button. Or select several in the Gallery and tap Delete.
  • A character: open your characters and delete the character. This also deletes the character's photos and every result made with it. Save anything you want to keep first.
  • An outfit or product photo: delete it from the outfit/product picker.

Deleted items are removed from our servers, not just hidden.

How do I report a result?

Open the result full screen and tap the ! (Report this result) button. Pick a reason and add a note if you like. We read every report.

Someone made content of me without my permission

Email support@joindupe.com with the subject "Likeness report". Tell us what you saw and where. We treat these as urgent, and we remove content and close accounts that break our rules.

Delete your account

  1. Cancel your subscription first (see below). Deleting your account does not stop Apple from billing you.
  2. In the app, open the menu, tap Delete Account and confirm.

What happens next:

  • Your account is switched off immediately. Nobody can sign in to it. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also remove Dupe's access to your Apple ID.
  • After 14 days, everything is permanently erased: photos, characters, results, captions, albums and credits. This cannot be undone.
  • Changed your mind? Email us within the 14 days and we can restore the account.
  • Want everything erased sooner? Email support@joindupe.com from your account's email address and ask for immediate erasure.

No access to the app? Email support@joindupe.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account" and we will do it for you.

Subscriptions and billing

How do I cancel my subscription?

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap Dupe.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription.

You can also get there from the app menu: Manage Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep your plan, and its credits, until the end of the period you have already paid for.

I deleted the app. Am I still being charged?

Yes, if you did not cancel. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription. Follow the steps above.

How do I restore my purchases on a new phone or after reinstalling?

  1. Install Dupe and sign in with the same account as before.
  2. Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with.
  3. Open the app menu and tap Restore Purchases.

Your characters and Gallery are stored in your account, so they come back when you sign in.

I paid but the app still asks me to pay

Dupe lets you pay before you create your account. Try these in order:

  1. Finish creating your account or signing in, then tap Restore Purchases.
  2. Check you are signed in to the account you paid with.
  3. Close the app fully and reopen it.

Still stuck? Email us with your Apple receipt (the order number is enough) and we will fix it. Please do not buy again.

How do I get a refund?

Apple takes the payment, so Apple decides refunds. We cannot refund you directly because we never hold your payment details. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose "Request a refund" and pick the purchase.

If something went wrong on our side, email us as well. We can return credits to your account and support your request.

How much does Dupe cost?

There are three plans: Weekly, Pro (monthly or yearly) and Max (monthly or yearly). Each gives you a set number of credits every period, and Max adds High quality. Subscribers can also buy one-off credit packs. The exact price in your country is always shown in the app and the App Store before you buy.

Is there a free trial?

No. You can look around and browse presets before you buy.

Account and sign-in

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and follow the email link. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.

Which sign-in should I use?

Always sign in the same way you signed up: Apple, Google, or email. Signing in a different way can create a separate, empty account. If your characters seem to have vanished, this is usually why. Sign out and try the other way.

Can I use Dupe offline?

You can browse your Gallery, characters and presets that have already loaded. Creating, buying, reporting and deleting need a connection.
