Dupe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

Dupe makes AI photos, carousels and short videos of a "character". Most characters are made from photos of the user's own face, so this policy explains, in plain English, what we collect, why, who it goes to and how you stay in control.

If anything here is unclear, email us at support@joindupe.com.

Dupe is run by David-Christian Green, trading as Unreal Studio, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom, at Apartment 55, 2 Langley Walk, Birmingham, B15 2EN, United Kingdom ("we", "us").

We are the "data controller" for your personal data. That means we decide how it is used and we are responsible for it.

2. The short version

  • Dupe is for adults (18+).
  • You give us photos. We use them only to make your characters and your results, and to check them for safety.
  • To do that, your photos and prompts are sent to the AI companies that run the models for us: WaveSpeed AI (image and video generation, using models from OpenAI, Alibaba and ByteDance) and Google (Gemini, for photo checks, prompt checks and prompt writing).
  • We do not use your photos, prompts or results to train AI models. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Nothing is uploaded or sent to AI until you agree on the AI sharing screen. You can withdraw that in the app.
  • Your results are private to your account. There is no public feed.
  • You can delete single results, whole characters, or your whole account inside the app. A deleted account is switched off at once and wiped for good after 14 days.

3.3 What crash reports contain

We set up crash reporting to avoid personal data. Before a report leaves your phone, the app:

  • removes email addresses, sign-in tokens, passwords and web links;
  • removes anything labelled as a prompt, photo, selfie or image link;
  • removes the user and request sections of the report entirely;
  • does not attach screenshots, screen recordings or the on-screen layout.

What is left is technical: device model, operating system version, app version, the error, and timing data for a sample of sessions. Our crash reporting service is set not to store IP addresses.

One limit you should know: reports of low-level ("native") crashes are made by a layer of the app below our filter. They contain technical crash data, not your photos.

  • We do not run face recognition to identify you or anyone else, and we do not build a database of faces.

3.6 Product analytics (PostHog)

To learn which parts of the app work and where people get stuck, the app can send usage events to PostHog, our product analytics service. It only does this if you agree. The first time you are in the app, it asks once: "Help improve Dupe? Share anonymous usage data", with Allow and Not now. Until you answer, and if you choose Not now, no usage events are sent or stored for sending.

  • What is sent: the name of an action (for example "onboarding step viewed", "paywall viewed", "purchase completed", "generation started", "result saved"), the name of the screen, and a few short technical details such as a plan or credit-pack product code, the type of media, a preset ID, a credit amount, how long a generation took, or a short error code. Basic device and app information is added: device model, operating system and app version, language and time zone.
  • Who it is linked to: a random ID that the app creates on your phone. Once you have an account, it is linked to your account ID (a random code, not your email). Your plan's product code, your phone's platform and the app version are stored with it. When you sign out or delete your account, the app starts a new random ID.
  • What is never sent: your email, your name, your photos or face, your prompts, captions, report notes or anything else you type. There are no screen recordings ("session replay") and no recording of your taps.
  • IP addresses: the app switches off location look-ups, and PostHog is set to discard IP addresses.
  • Changing your mind: open the menu and turn Share anonymous usage data on or off at any time. When it is off, the app sends no usage events from that phone. Your choice is kept on the phone.
  • Who processes it: PostHog Inc. (United States) processes this data for us, under a data processing agreement. See section 7 for how international transfers are protected.

We do not use this data for advertising, we do not combine it with data from other companies, and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.

4. Your photos, AI and safety checks

This is the most important part of this policy.

4.1 What happens to your photos

  1. You pick 3 photos and tick the two boxes (they are of you or you have permission; you are 18 or over). Nothing is uploaded until you have accepted the AI sharing notice and have an account.
  2. The photos are uploaded to our private storage (hosted by Supabase).
  3. The photos are sent to Google's Gemini AI, which checks that each one shows one clear, real face and nothing unsafe (see 4.3). A photo that fails is rejected and deleted from our storage.
  4. Gemini writes a short description of the character's appearance, which we store with the character.
  5. We join your photos into one reference picture. Each time you create something, that reference picture is sent to the image or video model at WaveSpeed AI together with the instructions for the shot, plus any outfit, product or Recreate photo you added.
  6. The finished result is copied into our private storage and appears in your gallery. Only you can see it.

Outfit, product and Recreate photos go through a similar Gemini check before they can be used. A face in those photos is fine (for example someone wearing the outfit).

If you invent a character in Character Studio, no photo of you is used. The character is made from the traits you choose.

4.2 What we do not do

  • We do not train AI models on your photos, prompts or results. The app does not build a custom AI model of you. Your photos are used as reference pictures at the moment each result is made.
  • We do not sell or rent your photos to anyone.
  • We do not show your photos or results to other users, and we do not use them in our marketing without asking you first.

4.3 Safety checks

To keep Dupe safe, we use automatic checks:

  • Photos you upload (character photos, outfit and product photos, Recreate photos) are checked by AI for sexual content, sexual content involving minors, violence and hate. Character photos are also checked for harassment, and must show one clear, real human face (not a drawing). A photo that fails is rejected and deleted.
  • Prompts you type (your own ideas and edit instructions) are checked by AI before any credits are taken. A prompt is blocked if it asks for sexual content, anything sexual involving a minor, intimate images of another real person, hate or harassment, graphic violence, illegal activity, or pretending to be a named real person or brand. The app tells you why it was blocked.
  • Repeat blocked prompts. After 5 blocked prompts in 24 hours we show a warning. After 10 in 7 days, typed prompts are paused for 24 hours (presets keep working). A prompt blocked for sexual content involving a minor suspends the account straight away. Nothing is deleted automatically. You can contact us if you think we got it wrong.
  • The AI models' own filters. WaveSpeed and the model makers also run their own safety filters. If one blocks a result, the generation fails and your credits are returned.
  • Reports. You can report any result from the full-screen view. We read reports and act on them.

These checks are automated and make mistakes. If you think a photo or prompt was blocked wrongly, or your account was paused or suspended wrongly, email support@joindupe.com and a person will look at it.

4.4 Your permission

Before the first upload or AI request, the app shows an AI sharing notice and asks you to agree. Your answer is saved on our server against your account, so it applies on every device, and our server refuses uploads and AI requests without it.

You can withdraw permission at any time from the app menu: AI Photo Sharing. When you do:

  • new uploads and new AI requests stop, on all your devices;
  • you can still open the app and see your gallery;
  • jobs already running may finish;
  • data already sent to an AI provider is not recalled;
  • your existing photos and media are not deleted automatically. Use the delete controls or Delete Account for that.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account inside the app: open the menu and tap Delete Account.

What happens:

  1. Straight away: your account is switched off. Nobody can sign in to it and nothing new can be made. If you signed in with Apple, we ask Apple to remove Dupe's access to your Apple ID.
  2. For 14 days: your data is kept, but locked. If you deleted your account by mistake, email support@joindupe.com within this time and we can restore it.
  3. After 14 days: everything is permanently erased in our daily clean-up. This includes your photos, characters, outfit, product and Recreate photos, generated media, captions, albums, credits, push tokens, app activity and your sign-in record. We also ask RevenueCat to delete its record of you, and PostHog to delete your analytics profile and usage events. This cannot be undone.

Want it gone sooner? Email support@joindupe.com from the address on your account and ask for immediate erasure.

Things to know:

  • Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Cancel there first (see our Support page).
  • Unused credits are lost when the account is erased.
  • Purchase records held by Apple, and data already sent to AI providers, are not erased by our deletion. Those companies keep them under their own policies.

10. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • stop or limit how we use your data;
  • move your data to you or another service in a common format;
  • withdraw consent you have given, including AI sharing permission.

You can also object to uses based on our legitimate interests, such as app activity analytics. Product analytics only run with your consent, and you can withdraw it yourself: turn off Share anonymous usage data in the app menu.

To use any of these rights, email support@joindupe.com from the address on your account. It is free. We reply within one month (45 days for California requests). We may need to check it is really you.

Much of this you can do yourself in the app: delete results, characters or your whole account, download your results, and withdraw AI sharing permission.

Complaints. We would like the chance to fix a problem first, so please contact us. You also have the right to complain to a regulator:

  • UK: the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk
  • EU/EEA: your local data protection authority

11. Data stored on your phone

To make the app fast and usable without a connection, the app keeps some data on your device:

  • your sign-in session, in your phone's secure storage;
  • a cache of your recent gallery items, your characters, presets and your credit balance, so you can browse when offline;
  • photos you picked during setup, until setup finishes;
  • your choice for Share anonymous usage data, the random analytics ID and any usage events waiting to be sent to PostHog.

This cached data is tied to your account. If someone else signs in on the same phone, the app does not show them the previous account's private data.

The media cache is protected by your phone's own security, not by extra encryption from us. You can clear it at any time from the app menu: Clear Image Cache. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Anything you saved to your phone's photo library stays there until you delete it yourself.

12. Security

We protect your data with measures including:

  • encrypted connections between the app and our servers;
  • private file storage, with short-lived links to view your own files;
  • database rules so each account can only reach its own data;
  • server-side checks that a photo belongs to you before it is used;
  • sign-in sessions kept in your phone's secure storage;
  • AI provider keys kept on our servers, never in the app.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

13. Age limit

Dupe is for adults. You must be 18 or over to use it. The app asks you to confirm this before you add photos. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and you must not upload photos of anyone under 18.

If you believe a child has used the app, or that a photo of a child has been uploaded, email support@joindupe.com and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. If the change affects which companies receive your face photos, the app will ask for your permission again.
