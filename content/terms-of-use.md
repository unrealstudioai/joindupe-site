## 2. What Dupe does

You make a **character**, either from 3 photos of yourself or by inventing one in **Character Studio**. You then pick a style (a "preset") or type your own idea, and the app makes AI photos, photo carousels and short videos of that character. You can also:

- **Recreate** a photo you upload: the app makes a new version of that scene with your character in it;
- **Edit** one of your results by describing a change;
- add an **outfit** or **product** photo so it appears in the result;
- add **captions** to your results.

Everything the app makes is **generated by AI**. It is not a real photograph or a real recording. Results vary. They may not look exactly like you and may contain mistakes or odd details.

## 6. Safety checks, limits and enforcement

We use automated checks, and human review of reports, to keep the app safe.

- **Photos are checked.** Character, outfit, product and Recreate photos are checked automatically before they can be used. A photo that fails is rejected and deleted.
- **Typed prompts are checked before you pay.** If a prompt or edit instruction breaks these rules, it is blocked, no credits are taken, and the app tells you why.
- **Repeat blocked prompts.** After 5 blocked prompts in 24 hours you will see a warning. After 10 in 7 days, typed prompts are paused for 24 hours (presets still work). A prompt blocked for sexual content involving a minor suspends the account immediately.
- **AI providers' filters.** Our AI providers run their own safety filters. If a filter blocks a result, the generation fails and your credits are returned.
- **Reports.** You can report any result from the full-screen view. We review reports.
- **Limits.** To keep the service fair and affordable, there are limits on every account, with or without a plan: up to 10 characters at a time, up to 10 new characters from photos in any 24 hours, and limits on how many photos can be checked and uploaded each day. The app tells you when you reach one.

- We may report illegal content, especially child sexual abuse material, to the police or the relevant authorities, and keep the evidence needed to do so.

Automated checks make mistakes. If you think we got it wrong, email [support@joindupe.com](mailto:support@joindupe.com) and a person will look at it.

If we close your account for a serious breach (for example sexual content involving minors, or content made of someone without permission), you lose any unused credits and are not entitled to a refund, except where the law says otherwise.
